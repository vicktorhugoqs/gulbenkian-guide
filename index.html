Gulbenkian · Founder's Collection

A field guide, reopening day

The Founder's Collection reopens 18 July 2026 after a 16-month renovation, in the Foundation's 70th year. New: a numismatics room, restored daylight and garden views from the galleries. Expect crowds — the route below follows the museum's own circuit, from ancient Egypt to Lalique, through 42 works with reasons to care about each.

One idea to carry through: Calouste Gulbenkian bought about 6,000 objects in his life and kept perhaps the world's highest ratio of masterpieces among them — "only the best". Watch the provenances: Hermitage sales, Rothschild collections, Howard Carter as a buying agent, Lalique as a personal friend.

Hours 10:00–18:00, closed Tuesdays
